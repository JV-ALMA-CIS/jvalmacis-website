About JV ALMA C.I.S

Founded in 2017, JV ALMA C.I.S is a multi-sector group based in Kenya, with its head office at King'ara Heights, Nairobi, and a branch office at Manipur Plaza, Mombasa.

Business units

  • Construction: facility management and renovations.
  • Agribusiness: infrastructure, training and digitization, including Kilimo Mkononi and CoffeeCore.
  • Oil & Gas Services: inspection and regulatory compliance.
  • IT Division: software development and digital platforms, including AlmaWorks, NyumbaSmart, AlmaSafety and AlmaHub.
